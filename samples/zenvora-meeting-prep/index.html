The business interpretation

  • Orvexa is the decisive driver. Its 1,200-tsubo unit returns to rent-paying occupancy from October 2026. The five-year NOI gain is 2,958.9, more than the entire portfolio’s net improvement.
  • Rent negotiations are mixed. Velmora and Kirevo are below their original forecast rents. Nuvessa improves modestly. Tavreno is unchanged.
  • Financing gets more expensive. Nuvessa and Kirevo each add 65 basis points to refinancing rates, raising annual interest by 86.7 from FY2026.
  • Only 20% of incremental net income stays in cash. With an unchanged 80% payout assumption, five-year extra net income of 1,664.0 produces 332.8 more closing cash by FY2030 and 1,331.2 more base-case dividends.

What management should highlight

The forecast is stronger conditional on the Orvexa letting. Confirm the counterparty, signed terms, rent commencement, incentives and delivery costs before treating the uplift as secured.

Without Orvexa’s incremental rent, the other v2 changes would reduce modeled FY2026 net income by approximately 72.0 versus v1, and FY2030 net income by 93.0. This is a mechanical sensitivity using unchanged tax and expense rules.

6. New property fund setup strategy and shareholder reporting

Zenvora plans to sell Velmora and Orvexa to a newly established Zenvora Property Fund at FY2026 year-end, retain a 10% GP equity stake and continue managing the assets for a fee. LP investors hold the remaining 90%. From FY2027, the properties' rental NOI leaves Zenvora's directly owned corporate portfolio, so the lease-up benefit no longer lifts the property-owner base case in the same way. The strategy assumptions, sale prices, acquisition pipeline, fees and distributions are unchanged between v1 and v2.

How to read this section: Fund-level figures describe the property fund as a whole. Zenvora-level figures describe its 10% GP investment, management fees and GP distributions. Corporate funding describes borrowing by Zenvora to finance future GP investments and is separate from the fund's own debt.

7. Important items to resolve before relying on the forecast

These issues were distinguished from the new submission changes. They were identified in the source files and are not repairs made to the workbooks.

High priority

Zenvora's corporate leverage excludes borrowing for future GP investments

Pre-existing calculation definition issue

Scenario!L63 is a Zenvora-level metric that divides remaining property debt of 3,569.1 by Zenvora equity of 106,289.4, displaying debt/equity of 0.034×. Adding the separately recorded corporate borrowing of 78,578.7 gives total Zenvora debt of 82,147.8 and total debt/equity of approximately 0.773×. Liabilities do include the corporate borrowing, so the balance sheet balances while the ratio understates Zenvora's total corporate leverage. This is not the fund-level debt/equity ratio.

Source: Forecast v1/v2, Scenario!L35, L37, L40, L63, L95

High priority

Zenvora coverage and interest-rate labels can mislead

Pre-existing metric definition issue

FY2030 NOI / interest is 0.890× and the displayed effective rate is 58.35%. These are Zenvora-level indicators, not fund-level metrics. The coverage numerator excludes AM fees and GP distributions; the rate denominator excludes corporate borrowing while the numerator includes its interest. Interest divided by total closing Zenvora debt is approximately 2.54%, a simple alternative ratio, not a time-weighted funding rate. Define recurring cash earnings and consistent debt denominators before using these as corporate financing indicators.

Source: Forecast v1/v2, Scenario!L16, L19:L23, L35, L65:L68, L95:L96

High priority

FY2026 disposal accounting uses inconsistent timing

Pre-existing model convention requiring confirmation

The new fund strategy case includes a full year of office NOI, removes 2,149.2 of office depreciation in that same year, and uses FY2025 property book values for a stated FY2026 year-end disposal. Reconcile depreciation, sale-date book value, disposal gain and tax together. Do not interpret the elevated FY2026 strategy-case profit as recurring earnings.

Source: Forecast v1/v2, Scenario!H6:H7, H17, H24, B85:B86; Fund AM!D5, E11:F13; Property Valuation!G9:G10
