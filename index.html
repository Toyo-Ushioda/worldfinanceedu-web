Modelling that holds up under scrutiny
Structured, auditable models that actually get used in decisions — not just built to satisfy a deliverable.
ii.
Presentation that changes the room
Translating numbers into the structured argument the audience needs to act on.

Business context, not just spreadsheets
Knowing where the numbers come from, why they move, and what to ask the rest of the business.

03 Practice 03 of 03 · Investment
09
Beyond training and CFO services

WFE also invests in startups.

WFE invests its own capital directly into early-stage startups at pre-seed and seed stage. To date, WFE has backed 10+ startups directly and holds stakes in 2 VC funds covering 40+ startups. Each investment is personal and conviction-driven, focused on the founding team and the problem they are solving.

  • 01
    Pre-seed and seed only
    Stage focus is pre-seed and seed — the points where founder selection, network and judgement matter most relative to capital.
  • 02
    Sector-agnostic, founder-led
    Sector is open. The decision is driven by the founding team and the problem they are solving, not by category preferences.
  • 03
    Post-investment support
    Startups backed by WFE get access to WFE's finance and operating experience, separate from any paid engagement.
Regulatory note

WFE invests its own capital as a sole proprietorship. WFE does not solicit, pool, or manage capital from third parties.

WFE does not provide investment advice, deal sourcing, fund management, or any other regulated financial activity, and is not licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) for any such activity.

Nothing on this page constitutes a solicitation or offer of securities, or an invitation to invest alongside WFE.
